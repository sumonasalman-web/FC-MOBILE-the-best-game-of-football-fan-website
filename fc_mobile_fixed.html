ABOUT FC MOBILE

A fan-made page for football and FC Mobile fans.

EA SPORTS FC Mobile is a football simulation mobile game developed by Electronic Arts (EA). It was previously known as FIFA Mobile and was rebranded as FC Mobile in September 2023.

Key Features

  • Ultimate Team: Collect football players and build your dream squad.
  • Head to Head (H2H): Play matches against other players.
  • VS Attack (VSA): Take part in quick attacking challenges.
  • Manager Mode: Choose tactics and let your team play.
  • PlayStyles: Explore special player abilities and traits.
  • Customization: Personalize parts of your team and its appearance.
